§ 01

The mechanism

Current AI systems are optimizers with hidden costs. They enhance the individual while the ripple effects go unmeasured. I make those externalities visible — and locate where to intervene for safer, more beneficial deployment.

Colleagueinherits work they never saw produced
Patientreceives a decision shaped by unseen assistance
Decision-makeracts on AI-generated content downstream
Teamshared understanding quietly fragments
Institutionexpertise is gradually displaced

An AI that boosts your productivity while fragmenting your team’s shared understanding is a local optimizer with hidden costs. I bridge AI, cognitive science, social science, and system design to measure these effects and build systems that augment entire human networks — not just individual users.
