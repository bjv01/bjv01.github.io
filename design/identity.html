§ 01

The Mark

Primary · the matrix-M

Filled cells trace an M — Moreno, matrix — inside its square. One cell is Sociometric Red: the externality, the one who wasn’t present.

Construction · 5 × 5

Built on a five-cell lattice. The clear space equals one cell on every side; never place the mark tighter.

Reversed

On ink, the fills become paper. The red holds. This is the avatar and favicon lockup.

§ 02

The Sociomatrix

The signature motif

The research made into an instrument. A pulse begins at one node — a single human–AI interaction — and fills the lattice outward. Every square it reaches is a person the effect touched: a colleague, a patient, a decision-maker who was never in the room.

source ▪ human + AI  ·  reached 0 nodes  ·  1 unmeasured
§ 03

Colour

The One Red Rule.  Sociometric Red appears exactly once per view — on the single element carrying the “hidden cost made visible” meaning. A second red anywhere is a bug.
The Fill-Not-Hue Rule.  State is shown by filling a cell solid ink (or inverting it), never by tinting. Colour is meaning, not decoration.

§ 05

Grid & Form

Open lattice

The idle state. Pierced cells, 1px silver rule.

Filled state

Effects landing. Solid ink; one red.

Zero radius

Square corners, flat surfaces. No rounding, no shadow — ever.

§ 06

Voice

Precise, first-person, unhurried. Claims are specific and stand without hype. The register is a scholar explaining an idea to a peer, not a brand selling one.

Navigation and labels keep the existing lowercase command grammar (_me, _research), set as letterspaced capitals — the one thread carried from the old site into the new world.

“Most AI evaluation optimizes the individual interaction. I measure what happens to the network after it.”

§ 08

Do & Don’t

Do

  • Align everything to the square lattice.
  • Show state by filling or inverting a cell.
  • Keep red to one element per view.
  • Set titles in letterspaced Josefin caps; read in Spectral.
  • Keep corners at 0 and surfaces flat.

Don’t

  • Use a cream ground or a serif display (the academic default).
  • Return to the dark-neon “techy” look.
  • Add a second accent, a gradient, or a rounded corner.
  • Set body copy in Josefin or a title in Spectral.
  • Float cells on shadows to fake depth.
