CThe Offprintgains: scholarly authority · risks: quieter
Villegas MorenoExternalities of human–AI interactionCambridge · 2026

Making the hidden social costs of AI visible

Bernardo Villegas Moreno — Center for Human-Inspired AI, University of Cambridge

A single human–AI interaction doesn’t stay put. It spreads to colleagues, patients, and decision-makers who were never present¹ — and the downstream consequences go unmeasured. This work makes those externalities visible and locates where to intervene.

Current systems optimize the individual interaction; organizational outcomes depend on what happens after it². I bridge social science, cognitive science, and system design to measure effects on entire networks, not single users.

¹ the colleague, the patient, the decision-maker.   ² trust, shared understanding, displaced expertise.

The offprint / journal page: the peer-reviewed page as the interface — running heads, an abstract, a footnote apparatus where the hidden effects live. Reads as pure scholarship (echoes your journal, Theorein).
