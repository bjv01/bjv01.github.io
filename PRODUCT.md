## Users

## Product Purpose

A personal brand — an identity system and the website that flagships it — for Bernardo Villegas Moreno. It exists to establish him among expert peers as the author of a distinct, credible research program on the social externalities of human–AI interaction, and to move a reader from understanding the idea to reaching out (collaborate / cite / contact). Success = a first-time expert visitor can, within seconds, name the program and take it seriously.

## Positioning

A **sociologist working inside AI** who measures the *downstream social consequences* of human–AI interaction — the externalities and ripple effects that land on colleagues, patients, and decision-makers who weren't present. The claim a neighbor can't truthfully copy: he treats AI as a social intervention with unmeasured second-order costs, fusing sociology, cognitive science, and AI/system design to make those hidden costs visible and locate intervention points. Most AI-evaluation work optimizes the individual interaction; his measures what happens to the human network after it.

## Product Principles

1. **Make the invisible visible.** The identity should dramatize hidden, second-order social effects — the core of his thesis.
2. **Social science is the lens, not decoration.** Root the world in the social-science intellectual tradition, not a generic academic-serif or tech default.
3. **Credibility for experts.** Rigor and restraint outrank spectacle; peers must take it seriously on first read.
4. **The work leads; the person authors it.** Research program foreground, biography in support.
5. **One portable system.** Mark, palette, type, and motif must carry across site, slides, CV, and social — not just one hero.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reading-heavy expert audience: prioritize sustained legibility and WCAG AA contrast in every theme. Any signature visual (diagram/network/data motif) must have an accessible, non-color-dependent reading and a reduced-motion fallback.
