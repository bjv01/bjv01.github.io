§ 01

_me

My work sits at the intersection of social science and technology, guided by the belief that AI should serve the best interests of humanity.

I am a PhD candidate at the Centre for Human-Inspired Artificial Intelligence (TRACE Lab), University of Cambridge, and a member of Wolfson College. Previously General Secretary of FEUPE and President of FEUCE.

§ 02

Research

TRACE Lab, Cambridge · AI Safety Institute (UK)2025–2026
Lead Researcher

Experimental infrastructure for controlled behavioural studies of ripple AI effects: synchronous, multi-user designs measuring how AI assistance reshapes downstream knowledge transfer, trust calibration, and collaborative decision-making across participant dyads.

TRACE Lab, Cambridge · NHS2026
Lead Researcher

AI externalities in healthcare: how AI assistance to physicians propagates through clinical workflows to patient outcomes, where the person affected never used the system.

Secondthought · Cosmos Institute2026
Collaborator

A research project supported by the Cosmos Institute. Primary grantee: Joy Wang.

International Programme on AI Evaluation2025
Student Fellow · Safety Track

A framework for social evaluations of AI: the second- and third-order effects of deployment on human collaboration, and cross-cultural sociotechnical safety-evaluation methods.

University of Edinburgh · Alan Turing Institute2021–2024
Research Collaborator

An interaction-based metric for ML interpretability: whether users can calibrate trust, spot errors, and act on explanations. Interpretability emerges through sustained interaction, not as a fixed property of the model.

Pontificia Universidad Católica del Ecuador2019–2023
Research Associate

Two interdisciplinary projects: a multilevel longitudinal analysis of 2,000+ students’ performance trajectories (Journal of Hispanic Higher Education); and a network analysis of economic-elite concentration across Ecuador’s top 100 firms (Redes).
